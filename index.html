A little more about this tool

Your images stay on your device.
Background removal runs entirely in your browser. Images aren’t uploaded to a server or saved by this site. The model and runtime files are downloaded from IMG.LY on first use; that asset host receives ordinary network information, but not your image. Processing stays local.
Free, with no account or usage limits.
No sign-up, credits, or paid exports. The work runs on your device, so processing speed depends on your hardware.
A few quality-of-life improvements.
Paste an image to start processing immediately, copy the transparent PNG, and press Esc or R to reset for the next image. Upload and drag-and-drop work too.

Inspired by LoneTools’ background remover. This is a customized version of that idea with a few quality-of-life features, using the same IMG.LY background-removal library.
